# Homes.com

## Who actually controls these photos

Homes.com belongs to CoStar and pulls from MLS feeds, so the **listing brokerage** controls the source. It
also blocks automated lookups (us included), so you'll need to check it yourself.

## Steps

1. Search for your address on Homes.com yourself: {{listingLink}}
2. If photos of your home show up, send the message below to the listing agent named on it.
3. Then use Homes.com's contact page (<https://www.homes.com/contact-us/>) to ask for the photos to be removed
   from the off-market listing.

## Message you can send

```email
Subject: Please remove the photos of {{address}}

Hi,

I live at {{address}}. Photos of the inside of my home are still on Homes.com.

The listing isn't active. Please remove the photos, or at least the interior ones.

Thank you.
```
