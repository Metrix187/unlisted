# Google Street View

## Who actually controls these photos

Google does. These are Google's own photos of the street, not listing photos. You can ask it to blur your
house and Google will do it for free. The blur is permanent, even if you change your mind later, and it
covers the current and future Street View imagery at that spot.

## Steps

1. Open your address on Google Maps: {{listingLink}}
2. Drop into Street View in front of your home (drag the little yellow person onto the street, or click the
   street-level photo).
3. Click the **three dots** next to the address at the top left (or **Report a problem** at the bottom
   right), then **Report a problem**.
4. Line your house up inside the red box, choose **My home** as what you want blurred, fill in the reason and
   your email, and submit.
5. Google emails you when it's done. It can take a few days to a few weeks.

You can also request a blur through the Google Maps app on your phone the same way.
