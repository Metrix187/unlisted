# Google Street View

## Who actually controls these photos

This guide covers Google's Street View imagery. Only a homeowner or tenant can request a house blur, and
Google requires proof of address. A blur applied to an existing image is permanent. Google also offers a
separate request to stop blurring future imagery; that leaves existing and historical images blurred.
See [Google's blur instructions](https://support.google.com/maps/answer/15439776?hl=en) and
[future-imagery policy](https://support.google.com/maps/answer/18729904?hl=en).

## Steps

1. Open your address on Google Maps: {{listingLink}}
2. Drop into Street View in front of your home (drag the little yellow person onto the street, or click the
   street-level photo).
3. Click the **three dots** next to the address at the top left (or **Report a problem** at the bottom
   right), then **Report a problem**.
4. Center your home, choose **My home**, certify your authority, and complete the form. Upload your proof
   directly to Google. Follow its current document criteria: redact sensitive information, keeping only
   the address and document date visible. Government IDs and handwritten documents are not accepted.
5. Submit the request. Google reviews it and may email for more information or a status update.

You can also request a blur through the Google Maps app on your phone the same way.
