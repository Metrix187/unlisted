# Realtor.com

## Who actually controls these photos

Realtor.com is run by Move, Inc. and gets its listings straight from MLS feeds. Same chain as everywhere
else: photographer → listing brokerage → MLS → Realtor.com. The **listing brokerage** can get them pulled
at the source, and Realtor.com's support handles requests for off-market homes.

## Steps

1. Open it up: {{listingLink}}
2. If you own it, you can claim it under **My Home** (<https://www.realtor.com/myhome>). That gets you an
   owner view, though photo removal still tends to go through support.
3. Send the message below to the listing agent or brokerage.
4. Contact Realtor.com support (<https://support.realtor.com>) with the listing link and ask for the photos
   to be removed from the off-market listing.

## Message you can send

```email
Subject: Please remove the photos of {{address}}

Hi,

I live at {{address}}. Photos of the inside of my home are still on Realtor.com:
{{listingUrl}}

The listing has ended. Please remove the photos, or at least the interior ones.

Thank you.
```
